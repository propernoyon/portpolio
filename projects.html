01Manufacturing ERP2024 – 2026

Starline Group

Client
Starline Group
Industry
Manufacturing & industrial business
My role
Full-stack ERP developer

Project overview

I developed a comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system for Starline Group to manage its manufacturing and business operations from one centralized platform.

The system brings several business departments together, so day-to-day operations, business data and workflows are handled in a single place.

Key modules developed

  • Sales Management
  • Purchase Management
  • Inventory Management
  • Production Management
  • Human Resource Management (HRM)
  • Accounts & Finance
  • Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
  • Secondary Sales Management

Key contributions

  • Developed an integrated ERP platform covering eight major business modules.
  • Implemented centralized management of business and operational data.
  • Built workflows connecting sales, purchasing, inventory and production.
  • Built business management features that support daily operational activities.

Technology stack

  • PHP
  • Laravel
  • MySQL
  • JavaScript
  • Bootstrap
