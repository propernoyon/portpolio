About me

I'm Toriqul Islam Noyon, known online as Md Noyon. Most of my work is enterprise resource planning software for manufacturing and industrial businesses: one system where a company runs sales, purchasing, stock, production, accounts and HR.

Around the core modules I build the parts that make an ERP usable in practice: role-based access for different teams, real-time reports and PDF exports, email notifications, and screens that work on a phone as well as a desktop.

I have built ERP software with ERP.COM.BD and studied at Feni Government Computer Institute (2015–2019).
