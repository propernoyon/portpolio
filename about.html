What I do

Most of my work is enterprise resource planning software for manufacturing and industrial businesses: the systems a company uses every day to run sales, purchasing, stock, production, accounts and HR from one place.

Around the core modules I build the parts that make an ERP usable in practice: role-based access for different teams, real-time reports and PDF exports, email notifications, and responsive screens that work on a phone as well as a desktop.
